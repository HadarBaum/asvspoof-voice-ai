Human-or-AI Voice Detector

A bonafide-vs-spoof classifier trained on the ASVspoof2019 (LA) dataset: real human speech vs. text-to-speech / voice-conversion synthetic speech.

Built for the BIU Big Data and AI course project. Pipeline: MinIO (object store) → Spark (batch feature extraction) → scikit-learn classifier → Kafka + streaming enrichment → Elasticsearch. See docs/DESIGN.md and docs/EXPLANATION.md in the repo for the full write-up.
