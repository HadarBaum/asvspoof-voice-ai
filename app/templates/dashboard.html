Why the decision threshold matters: on this dataset spoof clips outnumber bonafide (human) ones roughly 9-to-1. A model can score high on overall accuracy while quietly failing almost all bonafide clips, simply by leaning toward the majority class - the table below shows this actually happening at the usual default threshold of 0.5. EER (the metric the ASVspoof challenge itself is scored on) picks the threshold where both error types are balanced instead, which is why it's used here instead of 0.5.
